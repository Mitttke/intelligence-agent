Apollo Lead Gen Pipeline

Hiring Signal Detection & Cross-Border Expansion Targeting

n8n Claude AI Apollo HubSpot

An agentic workflow that detects companies hiring outside their home country—a strong signal they need international expansion support. The system partitions Apollo searches by HQ country, filters for cross-border job postings, dedupes against HubSpot and a 90-day cooldown ledger, and delivers enriched leads with AI-drafted outreach emails to sales leaders.

  • Partitioned search guarantees every result is hiring outside its HQ country—no false positives from domestic hiring
  • Multi-layer dedupe: company name (pre-enrichment), domain (post-enrichment), email (contact-level), plus 90-day cooldown ledger
  • Claude drafts outreach emails with strict style contracts; code-side scrub catches AI-sounding phrases and flags drafts with [CHECK] tags
  • Read-only HubSpot integration builds trust; everything delivered via internal review digest, nothing sent to prospects automatically

Approach

Build vs Buy: I chose to build custom agentic workflows over buying enterprise intent-data platforms to maintain strict control over proprietary ICP logic, avoid per-seat licensing bloat, and integrate deeply with our specific Apollo/HubSpot stack.

LLM vs Deterministic Code: I use deterministic code for 90% of the pipeline (noise filtering, deduplication, date-window validation, size-band math) to save API costs and prevent hallucinations. The LLM is reserved strictly for semantic ICP matching and summarization.

Trust vs Automation: I chose a "read-only" CRM approach initially. Instead of letting the AI auto-create HubSpot records (which risks polluting the CRM with unvetted data), the system generates rich, context-heavy digests and tracking workbooks. This built trust with sales leaders before we considered automated CRM writes.
