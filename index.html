Apollo Lead Gen Pipeline

N8NCLAUDE AIAPOLLOHUBSPOT

Hiring signal detection and cross-border expansion targeting

An agentic workflow that detects companies hiring outside their home country, a strong signal they need international expansion support. The system partitions Apollo searches by HQ country, filters for cross-border job postings, dedupes against HubSpot and a 90-day cooldown ledger, and delivers enriched leads with AI-drafted outreach emails to sales leaders.

  • Partitioned search guarantees every result is hiring outside its HQ country, so domestic hiring never produces a false positive
  • Multi-layer dedupe: company name before enrichment, domain after enrichment, email at contact level, plus a 90-day cooldown ledger
  • Claude drafts outreach emails under a strict style contract, then a code-side scrub catches AI-sounding phrasing and flags drafts with [CHECK] tags
  • Read-only HubSpot integration builds trust. Everything lands in an internal review digest and nothing is sent to prospects automatically

ICP Market Movements

N8NCLAUDE AISHAREPOINTHUBSPOT

Competitive intelligence and trigger event routing

A continuous intelligence engine that scrubs 44 RSS feeds and news sources weekly for trigger events such as funding rounds, M&A, international expansion and leadership changes that match the ICP. Events are validated against Apollo enrichment data, checked against HubSpot for existing relationships and warm intro paths, and delivered as regional HTML digests with Word attachments.

  • 44 fetches per run: 23 publisher RSS seeds plus 21 Google News queries with regional locales, all fail-soft so one dead feed never kills the run
  • Code-level pre-filtering before any LLM call saves tokens and keeps noise out of the model context. The JSON contract is validated in code, not trusted
  • Apollo enrichment is the authority on headcount and HQ. A second code pass re-checks ICP matches with size tolerance bands and jurisdiction aliasing
  • SharePoint JSON ledger tracks every surfaced event with a 60-day cooldown. Fail-closed reads guarantee no duplicate signals reach sales
  • HubSpot metadata-only reads surface the line reps care about: who owns the account, when you last spoke, and who can make the intro

// APPROACH & TRADE-OFFS
01

Build vs buy

I built custom agentic workflows instead of buying an intent-data platform. The vendors sell generic signals and charge per seat. None of them understood an ICP defined by cross-border movement, and none of them could join the signal to our own CRM context.

02

LLM vs code

Deterministic code handles about 90 percent of the pipeline: noise filtering, dedupe, date windows, size-band math. That keeps token cost down and hallucinations out. The model gets one narrow job, semantic matching and summarisation, and code validates everything it returns.

03

Trust vs automation

The CRM stays read-only and nothing ever reaches a prospect without a human rewrite. Digests and tracking workbooks built trust with sales leaders first. Automated writes are a later step, earned by conversion data, not assumed on day one.
